# Sunday 22 November 2026 — one day, everything that happened: 35 stories, 7 regions

Entry for the make.fun bounty [news-image-20261122](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261122)
("Sum up 22 November's world news in one image", $100 pot).

One 1024×1024 one-day evidence board: **35 stories, 7 regions** — a giant
date masthead, a strip of 6 checkable story numbers (0 deals agreed at
COP31 · 03:00 objections deadline · 2 US bases targeted in Bahrain ·
16 crew rescued from Sparta II · 60 wounded in Kryvyi Rih · 1,671 killed
in Gaza since the 2025 truce), 3 night/fire leads + 10 mediums with drawn
scenes, a correct-geography world map pinning the 13 drawn stories, 22
briefs, a one-line BY THE CLOCK strip, and a DATE CHECK box in the
winners' form ("Wires read up to 06:26 UTC") that names its exclusions
instead of hiding them.

Leads: COP31 ends the day with NO DEAL — text tabled, no fossil-fuel line,
$120bn adaptation target dropped, approval session 06:00 UTC · Iran
strikes US bases in Bahrain, 2 Patriot radars claimed hit · Sparta II
ablaze in the Gulf of Finland, 16 crew rescued, still burning late
Saturday. Every story carries its source (Reuters / AP / AFP / SCMP /
BBC / …) and every number was verified against real reporting from
20–22 Nov 2026, re-checked at build time 06:26 UTC.

## Files

- `entry.png` — the image (1024×1024 PNG, 224,291 bytes)
- `main.ts` — entrypoint: copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`
  (Node 22, standard library only, no network)

## Run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
# -> out/entry.png
```
