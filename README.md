# Sunday 22 November 2026 — one day, everything that happened: 35 stories, 7 regions

Entry for the make.fun bounty [news-image-20261122](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261122)
("Sum up 22 November's world news in one image", $100 pot).

One 1024×1024 one-day evidence board: **35 stories, 7 regions** — drawn
as a serif newspaper broadsheet (cream paper, huge serif masthead, italic
red deck, coloured region segments, sentence-case serif headlines). A strip
of 6 checkable story numbers (~200 nations at COP31 · 03:00 GMT objection
deadline · 400+ drones fired at Ukraine · 110 missiles the same night ·
2 US bases targeted in Bahrain · 16 crew rescued from Sparta II), 3 leads
with red tags and status labels (REPORTED / CLAIM / ONGOING), 10 mediums
with drawn scenes and red banner tags, a correct-geography dotted world
map pinning the 13 drawn stories, 22 briefs, a BY THE CLOCK strip, and a
red-bordered DATE CHECK box ("Wires read up to 07:10 UTC") that names its
exclusions instead of hiding them.

Leads: **COP31 nears a final deal** (Antalya — text tabled, no
fossil-fuel line, approval session under way; no deal adopted as of
07:10 UTC) · **Russia's overnight drones kill two in Zelenskyy's home
city** (148 drones, 3 missiles, 60 hurt) · **Iran strikes US bases in
Bahrain** (2 Patriot radars hit, Iran claims). Below them: the burning
Russian ship Sparta II off Finland (16 crew rescued), Hong Kong's vote,
India's record Test total, and 7 more drawn stories. Every story carries
its source (Reuters / AP / AFP / BBC / …) and every number was verified
against real reporting from 20–22 Nov 2026, re-checked at build time
07:10 UTC.

## Files

- `entry.png` — the image (1024×1024 PNG, 237,959 bytes)
- `main.ts` — entrypoint: copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`
  (Node 22, standard library only, no network)

## Run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
# -> out/entry.png
```
